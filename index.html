Scan paper, merge PDFs, send it anywhere.

Point your phone camera at a document, straighten it, make the paper bright white, sign it, and turn it into one PDF. You can mix in pages from PDFs you already have. When it's ready, send it to iMessage, Discord or Instagram. Everything stays on your device.

SignDraw or type your signature and put it on any page
ShareOpens your phone's share sheet so you can pick an app
